1 · The Movie Companion ↗ browser extension

Pauses any movie at moments you choose and shows a conversation card — a kid question, a tap-quiz, and a note for you with the mythology behind the scene. Works on anything that plays video in the browser. No tracking, nothing leaves your computer.

⬇ Download the extension (.zip)
  1. Unzip it somewhere permanent.
  2. Open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder.
  4. Pin the icon, open your movie, press play.

Comes with three Moana sets: Discussion, Real Legends told at sea, and the Repair Lens. Switch sets and themes in the extension popup.

2 · Your movies ↗ your own purchases

Paste the link to where your family bought or streams each film (YouTube, Disney+, Netflix...). It is saved only in this browser — no accounts, nothing uploaded. The WATCH button opens your copy; have the companion pinned and it takes over from there.

These links are yours alone. The shared story materials never include links to purchased content — only to official, public pages.
